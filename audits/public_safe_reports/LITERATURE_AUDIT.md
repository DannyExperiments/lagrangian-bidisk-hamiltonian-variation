# Bounded literature audit

Checked 27 November 2026. Ahn and Kerman's preprint v3 Question 1.13 asks for a nonzero first derivative at the identity. In v3, Theorem 1.3 gives stationarity for toric convex bodies, Proposition 1.12 treats the bidisk under linear maps and excludes a unitary image of a toric body, and Proposition 1.14 gives a width decrease at a global endpoint. The last follows from Ramos's toric model and global extension. Ahn's 2025 dissertation Proposition 1.1 already uses such a global Hamiltonian extension after restriction. The sequel returns to the global inequality after Proposition 3.7. Those comparisons leave the derivative at the identity undetermined.

The bounded search found no earlier explicit nonzero first variation for the bidisk. The supporting maximum-differentiation and spherical moment steps use standard arguments. This audit does not establish absolute publication priority.
